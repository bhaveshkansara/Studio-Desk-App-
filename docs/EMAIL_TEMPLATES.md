## **HOW TO USE THESE**

1. **Manual sending:** Copy-paste and send via email service (Gmail, Mailchimp)
2. **Automation:** Set up email sequences in Mailchimp/Brevo
3. **Triggers:**
   - Welcome: On signup
   - Day 1, 3, 7, 14, 15: Auto-triggered by date
   - Monthly: Send on 1st of each month

---

## **KEY METRICS TO TRACK**

- Open rate (aim for 40%+)
- Click rate (aim for 10%+)
- Conversion rate to paid (aim for 30%+)
- Retention rate (aim for 80%+ month-to-month)
