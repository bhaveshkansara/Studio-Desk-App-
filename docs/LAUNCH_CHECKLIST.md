## **POST-LAUNCH OPTIMIZATIONS**

After Month 1, optimize based on data:

1. **Double down on what works**
   - Which content gets most engagement?
   - Which traffic source converts best?
   - Which feature do users love most?

2. **Improve weak areas**
   - Lower conversion rate channels
   - High drop-off features
   - Support topics

3. **Scale what works**
   - Increase paid ad spend on converting channels
   - Create more content in winning formats
   - Build partnerships with top referrers
